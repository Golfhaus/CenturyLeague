Two arms: `wet-5` at mean tolerance, and `wet-5-adverse-035` with focal cold tolerance 0 versus opposing 1, heat traits .5 and moderation .35; psychology disabled.

Primary: pooled paired adverse-minus-mean winner difference with conservative Bonferroni/Wilson discordance interval, z = NormalDist.inv_cdf(1-.05/4). Secondary: paired score margin, physical energy/fatigue, duration, discipline and both sides. Report four profile-specific primary intervals protected across four comparisons. At the earlier observed discordance proportions, the planning half-width is about 1.9 percentage points with this fixed sample; realized precision and whether the interval fits the approximate eight-point review level are not guaranteed. No screening outcomes are pooled.

Continuation gate, fixed before execution: the fresh primary winner interval's upper endpoint must be below zero; all full-ledger/input/score/statistics/clock/source audits and independent recomputation must pass. This confirms a reproducible adverse model effect, not acceptability of its strength or adoption of .35. Report any interval extending beyond the review level. If the gate fails, do not reroll, expand or run the dependent stage. If it passes, proceed automatically with the stage below, as authorized.

## Stage 2: implemented combined-modifier screen

Study `copied-club-combined-modifiers-001`: **256 new matched seeds per four pairings**, split 128/128 across sides: **1,024 fixture clusters × 11 arms = 11,264 outcomes**. The sample is fixed as an initial interaction screen, not a high-precision ceiling certification. Use distinct stage/study seed namespaces; never pool either earlier screen or confirmation data. Original tactics, healthy availability, same fixed date and end policy.

Arms: neutral dry18 control; psychology favored/adverse alone; hot/cold physical favored/adverse corners; and each physical corner plus matching favored/adverse psychology. Hot corner: 35 C, 80% humidity, 35/50 km/h wind/gusts, 5 mm/h rain, 45 degrees. Cold corner: the confirmed -5 C/90% regime with the same wind/rain. Opposed extreme tolerance uses .35 only for the relevant heat/cold trait, with the other trait .5. Both environments are shared; traits change only extra thermal fatigue/recovery. Favored psychology gives focal +5 wins, opponent -5 losses and focal home; adverse reverses these. Confidence, frustration and home support use the existing bounded shared policy and do not modify player ratings. Neutral control remains exact v1.0 parity.

Primary family: four combined corners (hot/cold × favored/adverse) minus neutral control, with conservative paired Bonferroni/Wilson family protection across four contrasts, z = NormalDist.inv_cdf(1-.05/16). Pooled direction refers to the consistently designated focal intervention on these four pairings, not a league-representative effect. Report controls and all profile/side effects; do not pool arbitrary shared-weather-only focal effects. Secondary per-contrast nominal intervals: physical corners minus control; psychology alone minus control; adding psychology to identical physical conditions; and difference-of-differences interactions (combined minus physical minus psychology-only plus control). Interactions use fixture-level paired means and approximate normal intervals because winner interactions can take values -2 to +2. Do not add effects from separate studies to predict the combined result.

Report win rates, changed winners, score margins, duration, both clubs' active/final energy, shots, foul and serious-foul denominators, shared-psychology contributions and cap saturation. Compare total corners with provisional ten/fifteen-point review levels, retaining uncertainty. Per-profile and per-side checks are exploratory nominal except optional clearly labeled family-adjusted primary subsets; no best-case selection. No winner clamp, forced parity, fixture replacement, outcome-driven stopping, coefficient changes or automatic adoption. These four specified corners do not search all weather directions/extremes, roster qualities, all league matchups, histories or halftime policies.
