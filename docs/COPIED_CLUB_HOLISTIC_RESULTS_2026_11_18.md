## Design and scope

Copied 18 clubs with 35 players each, unchanged ratings and coach tactics, and 32 accepted nonvoided competitive tournament results. Six chronological history states preceded the first copied result and followed completed match dates 3, 6, 10, 13 and 17 November. Ordinary cases used actual streaks, ranging from -3 to +3. Stress cases retained specified opposing ±5 streaks and all-squad 1 vs 0 thermal fit; these traits are counterfactual inputs, not observed club physiology. Current copied rosters were used across historic streak states; these are not historical fixture reruns.

Every case sampled uniformly with replacement from 306 ordered distinct-club pairs, six history prefixes and two original simulation orientations, using separate unbiased SHA256 domains. The target includes those draws and does not condition on realized group counts. Six co-primary intervals use 24 one-sided Clopper-Pearson tails at 0.05/24, followed by signed subtraction. Beta inverse endpoints were independently verified using log-space binomial-CDF bisection. Prior synthetic samples have a different target and were not pooled.
