# Engine v2 scope and season rollout

Decision recorded 17 November 2026 from the project owner's explicit instructions.

## Publication authorization

The owner authorized pushing the prepared research changes to a separate branch in `Golfhaus/CenturyLeague`: `lab/drop-counter-recovery-2026-11-17`. This preserves the restored broader laboratory work, the newly qualified continuation adapter, study harnesses, plans and verification evidence. The initial automatic publication rejection preceded this explicit authorization. Historical study reports and proof files retain their original publication-status statements as records of that earlier moment.

Branch publication is a research checkpoint. Official engine adoption and activation remain separate release decisions after the gates below pass. This publication does not merge into main, deploy an application, change a scheduled official runner or alter competition data.

## Release boundary

- Keep the current engine and its existing configuration/source pins for the entire tournament now in progress.
- Target v2 for matches in the December 2026 season, after the halftime coaching policy is settled and holistic validation passes.
- Select and pin the engine by competition/season. A calendar date alone must not switch the engine for an unfinished tournament. If competitions overlap, the existing tournament retains its existing engine while an explicitly released new season can use v2.
- Preserve completed matches, source identities and historical results. A later release must not silently rerun or relabel them.
- Confirm the actual official tournament runner's current engine/configuration pins before integration; do not assume the recovered lab checkout identifies the currently deployed runner. This branch publication performs no live-runner configuration change.

## Scope for v2

Finish evaluation and integration of the already developed confidence, frustration, home support, weather, heat/cold tolerance and halftime coaching work, with the reliability, audit and replay infrastructure needed to operate them. Keep their agreed shared modifier budget and modest psychological influence as the evaluation framework. Existing review targets in the modifier-envelope plans remain provisional acceptance references, not clamps on match winners.

The current halftime implementation is a controlled continuation and intervention adapter. The automatic observation, action-ranking and adoption policy still requires restoration/integration and validation. Neither the single-slider screen nor the targeted drop-and-counter test constitutes acceptance of a complete coaching policy. The targeted package result remains inconclusive; current thresholds were not changed.

## Required gates before release

1. **Complete the halftime policy.** Specify the observations it can use, action packages, root-strategy constraints, workload/personnel handling, ranking and adoption rules. Retain Hold as a valid decision and record why each choice was made. Freeze the candidate before its new validation sample.
2. **Validate the policy on fresh data.** Check decision quality and frequency using natural first halves and varied game states, opponent quality, tactical backgrounds and player conditions. Keep earlier screens separate from confirmation. Do not choose or tune the policy solely to the winning cells of those screens.
3. **Pass holistic v2 testing.** Evaluate the included modifiers together, including ordinary conditions, favorable/adverse extremes, interaction and double-counting checks, and unequal-quality opponents. Verify that modifiers preserve uncertainty and do not make teams effectively invincible. Apply the agreed modifier-envelope criteria rather than inventing new limits during analysis.
4. **Pass an isolated season rehearsal.** Exercise the complete candidate, accumulating histories and operational workflow without official writes. Audit scoring, clocks, fatigue/recovery, eligibility, substitutions and statistics, with reproducible source/configuration/seed identities and qualified runtime/replay checks.
5. **Prepare and approve the release.** Record an immutable v2 source artifact, configuration, validation evidence and the intended December season identifier. Verify competition-scoped selection and rollback for future matches. Activate only after an explicit release decision; publication of the lab branch alone is not activation authorization.

## Deferred to a possible v3

Do not expand v2 to include chemistry, leadership, momentum, sharpness, opponent preparation, match significance, travel/recovery, expectations or additional pressure-sensitivity mechanisms. Existing tactical pressure remains part of current gameplay; the deferred item is the proposed additional pressure-response mechanism.

Revisit these additions after v2 has been used long enough to assess its behavior in practice. No v3 implementation or activation date is committed by this decision.
